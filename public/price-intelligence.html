SOURCE NOTE

Government data integration

TexHive is prepared to consume the Government of India's Open Government Data / AGMARKNET daily mandi-price resource. That source is daily-granularity and is published by the Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare. It is suitable for raw agricultural commodities such as cotton where the dataset provides a matching commodity/market record. Cotton yarn and finished-fabric reference prices should only be shown when a source actually publishes those items.

Source: data.gov.in / AGMARKNET. Verify the source, unit and date before using a price for a real commercial decision.
